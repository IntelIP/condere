# Preserved question guides: M7, B3, B6
Ported verbatim from the 26-question method (commit e26d4b59). Use them as checklists, not output fields.

## M7. How large is the realistically reachable opportunity?
- Decision: Estimate a realistically reachable opportunity within the chosen scope.
- Evidence needed: Eligible customer counts, exclusions, geography, period and a comparable spending basis, with explicit reach assumptions.
- Search routes:
  - Official statistics, registries or industry datasets matching the target segment and geography.
  - Original methodology behind population counts and the already reviewed spending evidence.
- Weak signals: A broad market headline or arbitrary capture percentage does not establish reachable demand. Counts from different periods or definitions are not interchangeable.
- Completion: Provide reproducible low/base/high ranges with units and assumptions, or identify unavailable required inputs without inventing a precise market value.
- Points to cover: eligible customers, exclusions, spending basis, reach assumptions
- Contract rule: Show geography, period and reproducible bottom-up ranges; capture rates are assumptions and unavailable inputs cannot become precise market values.

## B3. Can we serve customers profitably?
- Decision: Determine the conditions under which serving customers could be profitable.
- Evidence needed: Comparable revenue, usage, provider prices, variable costs, support, payment costs, fixed costs and acquisition assumptions.
- Search routes:
  - Reuse reviewed Technical operating requirements and Market pricing evidence.
  - Official provider and payment pricing for the relevant region and usage, plus measured receipts when available.
- Weak signals: A subscription price minus model costs alone does not establish profitability. Unknown support or acquisition costs cannot become zero.
- Completion: Calculate reproducible low/base/high scenarios and sensitivity with explicit units; block a profitability conclusion when required inputs remain unavailable.
- Points to cover: revenue basis, variable costs, fixed costs, acquisition costs, sensitivity
- Contract rule: Use reproducible low/base/high usage calculations; distinguish measured costs, prices and assumptions; unknown costs cannot establish profitability.

## B6. Can we build and operate the offering with our resources?
- Decision: Assess whether the founder can build and operate the proposed scope.
- Evidence needed: Reviewed Technical requirements, minimum capabilities, skills, dependencies, ongoing work, time and funding constraints.
- Search routes:
  - Reuse the Technical result and founder constraints before seeking new implementation references.
  - Official dependency, deployment and operating documentation for specific unresolved requirements.
- Weak signals: A quick prototype or framework example does not establish delivery time, operational reliability or total effort.
- Completion: Match a bounded delivery proposal to resources and dependencies; label time and effort estimates and preserve unavailable Technical inputs.
- Points to cover: minimum scope, skills, dependencies, time and funding, operating work
- Contract rule: Match sourced or estimated delivery needs to founder constraints and relevant Technical findings, retaining uncertainty.
