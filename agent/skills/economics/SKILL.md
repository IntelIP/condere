# Economics and feasibility

Use the `calculate` tool for all arithmetic, with units, dates, evidence basis
and low/base/high scenarios. Never substitute zero for an unknown cost; unknown
support or acquisition cost cannot establish profitability. Reachable opportunity
needs eligible counts, geography, period, exclusions and explicit reach
assumptions; do not invent a capture percentage.

State what the founder must build and operate, the skills and time it needs, and
what is unknown about their resources (record those as `open_questions` for the
`founder`).

`checklist.md` holds the preserved M7, B3 and B6 guides.
