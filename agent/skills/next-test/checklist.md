# Preserved question guides: B4, B8, B9
Ported verbatim from the 26-question method (commit e26d4b59). Use them as checklists, not output fields.

## B4. How could we acquire our first customers?
- Decision: Propose a reachable first-customer acquisition experiment.
- Evidence needed: Reviewed customer segment and buying path, an accessible channel, a concrete offer and bounded effort or cost assumptions.
- Search routes:
  - Reuse Market channel findings and inspect relevant public channel access or pricing terms.
  - Find comparable acquisition accounts only to inform assumptions, preserving their population and conditions.
- Weak signals: A large community or another company's conversion rate does not establish our acquisition cost.
- Completion: Propose a cohort, channel, offer and observable result; keep conversion assumptions provisional and do not contact anyone.
- Points to cover: initial cohort, channel, offer, effort and cost, observable result
- Contract rule: Propose a reachable acquisition experiment; conversion and acquisition costs remain unverified until measured.

## B8. What could make the opportunity unsuitable?
- Decision: Identify constraints that could make the opportunity unsuitable.
- Evidence needed: Applicable requirements and explicit founder constraints tied to a concrete product decision, including unresolved mandatory inputs.
- Search routes:
  - Reuse recorded constraints and inspect authoritative terms, regulations or requirements only where relevant to the offering.
  - Identify what information or expert input is needed to resolve a material constraint.
- Weak signals: Generic risk lists and speculative concerns do not establish a blocker; a material hard constraint cannot be averaged into a score.
- Completion: Explain the affected decision and evidence or input needed, distinguishing an established constraint from an unresolved risk.
- Points to cover: material constraints, affected decisions, required inputs
- Contract rule: Explain decision-relevant constraints and unmet hard requirements; do not average a blocking condition into a score.

## B9. What should we test next, and what result changes our decision?
- Decision: Choose the next bounded test that could change the human's decision.
- Evidence needed: Reviewed findings, required unresolved questions, the most important assumption and the founder's resource limits.
- Search routes:
  - Reuse the saved findings and identify the uncertainty that matters most to the next decision.
  - Seek only a specific missing fact needed to define a feasible test; unresolved customer evidence may require a proposed interview or purchasing experiment.
- Weak signals: More sources or higher agent confidence do not define an experiment's success. A recommendation is not permission to execute it.
- Completion: Propose a method with observable success, failure and inconclusive conditions and finite time/spend; preserve human review and do not execute the test.
- Points to cover: recommendation, critical assumption, method, success rule, failure rule, inconclusive rule, proposed limits
- Contract rule: Propose a bounded validation step and decision conditions; retain human review and do not execute the experiment.
