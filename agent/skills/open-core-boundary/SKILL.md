# Open-core boundary and options

Write two to four genuinely different `options`. Routes: `open_source_owned_ip`,
`open_core_on_existing_oss`, `managed_service`, `dual_license`, `services_only`,
`none`. Include doing nothing or a narrower route when evidence favours it.

For each option state the open part, the paid part, the buyer, why they pay,
the main risk, and the case for and against, each backed by claim IDs.

Draw the boundary by buyer: features an individual practitioner needs stay open;
what an organisation's buyer pays to have (operations, scale, security,
compliance, support, governance) is paid. Tie paid value to an observed operating
burden or buyer need, not to a competitor's packaging. State why a customer would
not just self-host the open part, and how easily a competitor or the upstream
project could copy the paid part.
