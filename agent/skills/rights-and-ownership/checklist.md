# Preserved question guides: T6
Ported verbatim from the 26-question method (commit e26d4b59). Use them as checklists, not output fields.

## T6. May we offer it as a hosted or open-core product?
- Decision: Determine whether licensing, contributor terms or trademarks permit a hosted or open-core offering.
- Evidence needed: LICENSE and NOTICE files, per-package license fields, contributor license agreements, trademark policies, and any source-available or field-of-use restrictions such as AGPL network clauses, SSPL or BSL.
- Search routes:
  - Read license files and license fields listed by the repository index.
  - Read the project's trademark, contribution and commercial-use policies from official sources.
- Weak signals: A permissive top-level license does not cover every bundled component; absence of a trademark policy is not permission to use the name.
- Completion: Quote the governing terms, explain their hosting implications, and list the questions that require legal review.
- Points to cover: license, hosting rights, contributor terms, trademark and branding, commercial constraints
- Contract rule: Quote the license and related terms; state hosting implications as interpretation, not legal advice, and identify questions that need counsel.
