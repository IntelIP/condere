# Buyer evidence

Fill `buyer` and the buyer claims behind every option. Use the `search-routing`
skill to choose sources; delegate reading many pages to `extractor` subagents,
which return claims with exact quotes.

Keep user, buyer and payer distinct. Attribute pain to the people observed;
frequency, severity and population claims need suitable scope. Separate
attention, adoption, purchase and retention. Compare against what solves the
same task today, including manual work, internal tools and doing nothing.
Feature requests do not prove switching intent. Advertised prices, actual
spending, stated willingness and commitments are different evidence. A competitor's
revenue does not prove willingness to pay for this offering.

Prefer recent firsthand accounts: practitioners on Reddit, Hacker News and X
describing the problem, workarounds and costs; review sites for buyer complaints;
pricing pages for current prices. Multiple pages repeating one account are one
origin group, not independent confirmation. A bounded search that found nothing
does not prove absence.
