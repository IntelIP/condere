# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and code constructively,
welcome different experience levels, and respect privacy and consent.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation and
publishing another person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces.
Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or ban accounts to protect
the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. If you do not have a private
contact channel, open an issue requesting one without naming affected people or
including incident details. Do not put sensitive reports in public threads.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other official
community spaces, and to people representing the project publicly.
